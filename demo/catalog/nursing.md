# Bachelor of Science in Nursing

Northfield University is a fictional university. This page is demonstration content.

## Admission to the program

The B.S. Nursing program is direct admit. A first-year applicant who is admitted to Northfield University as a nursing major starts in the nursing program in the first semester. There is no second application to the program after the first year.

Transfer applicants are admitted to nursing as seats allow, and must have completed anatomy and physiology with a grade of B or better.

## What you study

- Years one and two: anatomy and physiology, microbiology, chemistry, and foundations of nursing practice.
- Years three and four: clinical rotations in adult health, pediatrics, maternity, mental health and community health.
- The Simulation Lab, in Harper Hall, has six patient rooms with high-fidelity manikins. Nursing students use it from their first semester.

## Clinical placements

Clinical rotations take place at Northfield Regional Medical Center and four partner clinics. Students need their own transportation to clinical sites in years three and four.

## Licensure

Graduates are eligible to sit for the NCLEX-RN examination. Northfield University does not guarantee licensure or employment.
