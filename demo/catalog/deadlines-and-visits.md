# Application deadlines and visits

Northfield University is a fictional university. This page is demonstration content.

## First-year application deadlines

- Priority deadline: January 15. Applications received by this date are considered first for admission, scholarships and housing.
- Regular deadline: March 1.
- After March 1, applications are reviewed as space allows.

The nursing program fills early. Nursing applicants are encouraged to apply by the January 15 priority deadline.

## What a complete application needs

- The application form, through the Northfield application or the Common App.
- An official high school transcript.
- One essay of 250 to 650 words.
- The application fee of $50, or a fee waiver. A fee waiver is available to any applicant for whom the fee is a hardship: ask an admissions counselor.

Test scores are optional.

## Visiting campus

- Campus tours run Monday to Friday at 10 a.m. and 2 p.m.
- Open houses for admitted students are held on Saturdays in the spring. Each program hosts its own: nursing open houses include a tour of the Simulation Lab in Harper Hall and time with nursing faculty and current students.
- Sign up for an open house on its page. Space is limited, and sign-ups close a few days before each event.

## After you are admitted

- The enrollment deposit is $300 and is due May 1.
- Housing forms open after the deposit is received.
- Orientation is held in June and again in August.
