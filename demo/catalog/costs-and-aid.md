# Costs and financial aid

Northfield University is a fictional university. This page is demonstration content.

## Cost of attendance, per year

- Tuition: $38,400
- Fees: $1,250
- Housing and meals, on campus: $14,200
- Books and supplies, estimated: $1,100

Nursing students pay an additional clinical fee of $600 per year in years three and four.

## Financial aid

- Northfield University's FAFSA school code is 009999.
- The priority date for financial aid is February 1. Students who file the FAFSA by this date are considered first for institutional grants.
- An award letter lists grants and scholarships, which are not repaid, and loans, which are. The amount a family pays is the cost of attendance minus grants and scholarships.
- Some students are selected for verification and are asked for extra documents. Aid is not paid until verification is complete.

Northfield University does not quote an aid amount before a student has been admitted and has filed the FAFSA. An admissions counselor or the Office of Financial Aid can explain an award letter line by line.

## Merit scholarships

Every admitted first-year applicant is considered for merit scholarships. There is no separate form. Awards are listed in the admission letter.
